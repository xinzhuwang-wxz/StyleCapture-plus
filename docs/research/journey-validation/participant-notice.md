## Research purpose

StyleCapture Journey M0 is a paid problem validation study for one narrow job: whether adults with a real upcoming 3-7 day trip value a plan that uses their own garments to create day/activity Looks, alternatives, a deduplicated packing list, gaps, and weather revisions.

This is research, not a released App. The ¥12 refundable deposit is for the research concierge result only. It is not an App unlock, not an App Store product, and not an external iOS purchase link.

## Who can participate

Participants must confirm all of the following:

- 18 years or older.
- A real 3-7 day overnight trip departing in 7-30 days.
- At least 8 owned garments or accessories available for the trip; 12-30 are recommended for a stronger plan.
- Willing to show low-sensitivity trip proof with name, order number, ID, exact lodging, and contact details redacted.
- Not a team member, direct family member, duplicate subject, pro trialist, or ineligible non-ICP traveler.

## Minimum data collected

Collect only what is needed to qualify the participant, build the one trip plan, deliver/refund the research offer, and compute de-identified aggregate metrics:

- Eligibility answers: age confirmation, trip timing, trip length, and exclusion status.
- Redacted trip proof viewed only for qualification.
- Current behavior, pain score, cost of failure, and evidence of action.
- Trip constraints: destination or city-level weather context, dates, activities, dress code, walking, luggage, warmth, must-pack and excluded items.
- De-identified garment labels and attributes for this trip.
- Offer outcome: `paid`, `declined`, or `refunded`.
- De-identified evidence references for verified deposit/payment, refund, delivery, and post-trip outcome.
- Post-trip outcome after `trip_end+7d`, including non-response.

Do not collect full wardrobe history, unrelated photos, precise lodging, government ID, order number, full legal name, sensitive free text, or contacts beyond the minimum operational channel needed outside Git.

## Evidence viewing and deletion

Trip proof is viewed only to confirm the 7-30 day, 3-7 day travel eligibility. Participants should redact name, order number, ID, exact lodging, and contact details before showing it.

After qualification is recorded, the operator deletes proof only from local caches and files under operator control. If a third-party channel cannot be controlled or deletion cannot be verified, pause that channel and follow the notice and authorized legal subject process. The committed repository stores only de-identified eligibility and `evidence_ref` values.

## AI and human processing disclosure

The plan may be prepared by human operators with AI-assisted drafting, sorting, weather reasoning, or language generation. A human operator must review the plan before delivery.

The operator must not send photos, itinerary proof, contact details, payment proof, or unnecessary identifying free text to an AI service unless the legal subject has disclosed the processor, data category, purpose, retention, deletion path, training use, and any required consent path before use.

AI assistance does not guarantee a correct or suitable outfit result. Participants should review the plan against real weather, venue rules, luggage limits, comfort, safety, and personal judgment.
